A slash command shouldn't need a separate install. In nanocoder it's one `.md` in `.nanocoder/commands/`. Restart, it appears under `/`. Bundle with a subagent and tool as `.nanocoder/skills/<name>/`: one folder, `skill.yaml` plus `commands/`. Commit the folder.
